About Elettranova

Elettranova is a single-shop integrator of global engineering and consulting services, based in the Silicon Valley. Our team of experts includes highly trained scientists, engineers, analysts, and security advisors as well as business process, legal and regulatory affairs specialists. Our customers have a diverse range of needs and we work with each customer to design a dynamic and highly-skilled team to execute to their projects with diligence and excellence.

What we offer

From start-ups planning to complex engineering projects, we provide each customer with an independent and highly-customized solution. Our team of experts manage and control the execution of all project components, from inception to completion, and assure quality, functionality and on-time delivery.
