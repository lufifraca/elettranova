About Elettranova

Elettranova is a single-shop integrator of global engineering and consulting services, based in the Silicon Valley. Our team of experts includes highly trained scientists, engineers, analysts, and security advisors as well as business process, legal and regulatory affairs specialists. Our customers have a diverse range of needs and we work with each customer to design a dynamic and highly-skilled team to execute to their projects with diligence and excellence.

What we offer

From start-ups planning to complex engineering projects, we provide each customer with an independent and highly-customized solution. Our team of experts manage and control the execution of all project components, from inception to completion, and assure quality, functionality and on-time delivery.

Leadership

Fabio Ficano,
Founder & managing director

Fabio Ficano is Founder & President of Elettranova Engineering. Ficano is an eclectic entrepreneur, investor and tireless business leader specialized in clean/green technology, renewable energy, large infrastructure projects, security technology, transportation, biometrics, defense and global industry affairs.

Ficano is also Founder and President of LufraRise; a firm specialized in business development, government and industry affairs with particular emphasis on bi-lateral investments between U.S. and European enterprises. Ficano is also Co-founder and Managing Partner of Moncada Energy USA and Moncada Energy Malaysia & Philippines. Ficano is a member of the “U.S.–Italy Bilateral Climate and Energy Partnership”, and since 2014, has been a commissioner and judge for the U.S.-Italy Fulbright Best Commission.
